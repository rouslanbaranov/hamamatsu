01アルバム/The record

Two languages.
One record.

Summer Avenue is a record about distance, warm streets, and the hour after sunset.

The album moves between Japanese and English; both languages belong to the same world. Around them, the record holds its city-pop balance — bright surfaces, a quiet center, and room to breathe.

Rubar is a city-pop project built around the space between Japanese and English — and the quieter side of the city.

Summer Avenue is a record to hear from beginning to end. It treats Japanese and English as one language, and nostalgia as something you can move to.
